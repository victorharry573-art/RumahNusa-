About RumahNusa

RumahNusa helps people explore property styles and residential communities throughout Indonesia. Our goal is to make property information clear, welcoming and easy to view on mobile devices.

Public property pages will show the general city and country only. Exact street addresses will be shared privately by an agent with serious prospective customers.

Verified listings can display prices in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) and US dollars (USD).
